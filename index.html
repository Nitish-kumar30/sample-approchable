FAQ

Who is this for?
Anyone who wants practical AI skills — especially non-technical people, product teams, designers, marketers, and founders.
Do I need to code?
No. Our cohorts focus on no-code tools, vibe-coding approaches, prompt engineering, and small, practical projects you can finish in weeks.
What tools will we use?
A mix of no-code platforms, AI playgrounds, and simple notebooks depending on the cohort. Expect hands-on tutorials using approachable tools.
How does the AI assistant help?
The assistant sends reminders (WhatsApp/email), summarizes weekly sessions, creates quick quizzes and flashcards, and nudges members to submit exercises.
