FAQ

Who is this for?
Anyone who wants practical AI skills — technical people, non-tech people, product teams, designers, marketers, and founders.
Do I need to code?
No. Our cohorts focus on no-code tools, vibe-coding approaches, prompt engineering, and small, practical projects you can finish in weeks.
How long is the program?
4 weekly live sessions (90 mins each), followed by a 2–3 week independent capstone build period, and a final group session for project review and doubt clearing. Total: approximately 7–8 weeks from start to finish.
Why is the fee non-refundable?
We keep cohorts to 20 seats max. When someone commits a seat and doesn't show up, it takes the spot from someone who would've. The commitment fee protects the group's experience.
What tools will we use?
A mix of no-code platforms, AI playgrounds, and simple notebooks. Expect hands-on tutorials using approachable tools — ChatGPT, Claude, n8n, Lovable, and more.
What is the Approachable learning platform?
All enrolled students get access to the Approachable learners platform — a dedicated learning hub with quizzes, progress tracking, leaderboard, an interactive prompting guide, and sample prompts to practice between sessions.
How does the AI assistant help?
The assistant sends reminders (WhatsApp/email), summarizes weekly sessions, creates quick quizzes and flashcards, and nudges members to submit exercises.
